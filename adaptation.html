✦ EARLY STAGE

ADAPTATION ROOM

Bring stories to new forms.

Adaptation Room is MeteorInk's dedicated space for the next stage of a story: connecting strong written worlds with opportunities in animation, film, audio, games and other formats.

Discover Stories

Browse stories with the kind of characters, worlds and narrative foundations that could translate well beyond text.

Author First

Authors remain at the center of the process. Adaptation interest should build around creator ownership, consent and clear communication.

Build Beyond Text

Over time, this room can become a structured place for adaptation pitches, development conversations and creative partnerships.

How the room is intended to work

01 · DISCOVER

Readers and potential creative partners find stories that stand out.

02 · DEVELOP

Interested parties can discuss the format, creative direction and practical requirements.

03 · ADAPT

When the right opportunity exists, the adaptation moves forward through proper agreements and production channels.

The room is just getting started.

MeteorInk is still building the systems behind this section. For now, explore the library or contact the team if you have an adaptation-related proposal.
