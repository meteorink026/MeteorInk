# MeteorInk Age Policy

- Date of Birth is mandatory for standard registration.
- Future dates are rejected.
- The current standard account flow accepts users aged 18 or older.
- Users under 18 are not created through the standard flow. A future verified parental-consent flow and additional safeguards are required before allowing such accounts.
- This is a platform policy and should not be presented as a claim that Indian law imposes a 12+ cutoff.
- Production enforcement must be implemented server-side, not only in browser JavaScript.
- Content should use appropriate age/content ratings and access controls.
