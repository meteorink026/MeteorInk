Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

MeteorInk respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use the MeteorInk website and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MeteorInk, we may collect information such as your email address, account details, date of birth or age-related registration information, profile information, and information you voluntarily provide when publishing or interacting with content.

If you sign in with Google or X, we may receive information provided by the relevant identity provider that is necessary to authenticate your account, such as your provider account identifier, name, profile information, and email address where available and permitted.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create, authenticate, maintain, and secure your MeteorInk account.
  • To provide reading, writing, publishing, profile, and other platform features.
  • To associate a third-party sign-in account with your MeteorInk account.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and violations of our policies.
  • To understand website usage and improve the service.
  • To communicate with you about your account, service updates, or support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

MeteorInk may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, database infrastructure, analytics, advertising, and other operational purposes. These providers may process information according to their own policies and the permissions or configuration applicable to the service.

For example, Google and X may process information when you choose their sign-in options. Google Analytics may collect website usage information. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies where enabled.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

MeteorInk may use cookies, local storage, session technologies, and similar mechanisms to maintain authentication state, remember preferences, support functionality, and measure usage. Some features may not work correctly if required storage technologies are disabled.

5. User Content

Content you publish on MeteorInk may be publicly visible depending on the feature and visibility settings. Do not publish private information that you do not want other users to see. You remain responsible for the content you submit and for having the rights necessary to publish it.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, because apparently even computers occasionally enjoy creating problems.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may contact us to request account or personal-information deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

8. Children and Age Restrictions

MeteorInk's standard registration flow is currently intended for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly create standard accounts for users below that age through the standard registration flow.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MeteorInk develops or as our practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy or account-related questions, contact the MeteorInk team at business@meteorink.com.
